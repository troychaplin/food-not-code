# Food Not Code

## Salmon and Leek Sauce

**Serves:** 4
**Side Ideas:** Garlic mash and roasted asparagus / broccoli / other veggie medley
**Substitutes:** Rainbow trout can be used in place of salmon

### Fish

- Lightly drizzle salmon with oil and lightly salt and pepper
- Broil for 10-12 depending on thickness (my oven defaults to 550 on broil, we use that)

### Sauce

- While fish cooks, clean and slice leeks (roughly 3 leeks)
- Sautee garlic and shallots in butter until soft
- Stir in heavy cream (roughly a cup)
- Add zest of a lemon
  - For extra creamy sauce add half cup of grated parmesan
- Simmer until creamy and thick, salt and pepper to taste
  - Add a kick with some chilli flakes if desired

### Serve

- Plate salmon, spoon on generous amount of leek sauce
