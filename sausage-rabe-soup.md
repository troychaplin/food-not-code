# Food Not Code

## Sausage Rabe Soup

**Serves:** 8-12
**Side Ideas:** Sour dough crusty bread
**Substitutes:** Kale can be used in place of broccoli rabe

### Soup

- Dice and a large onion and 3-4 cloves of garlic
- Heat oil in a pot, brown onions and garlic
- Add sausage and fully cook, breaking into small pieces as you go
  - I use 2-4 large sausages with casing removed
  - Use a sausage with good flavour, italien or hot are ideal but exploring is fun
- Add 2-3 tablespoons of crushed fennel seed
  - Another change to make it your own by spicing up the sausages to your own liking
- Chop an entire bunch of brocoli rabe and mix it into the sausage and let it wilt
  - When wilting you can add a cup of the stock from the next step to star deglazing the pot as you stir
  - **See soup addon below:** this is where you would add potatoes if desired
- Increase the heat to medium-high, add a total of 4-6 cups of chicken stock
- Bring to a boil, reduce and simmer uncovered for 20-25 min
  - **See soup addon below:** this is where you would prep the pasta if desired
- Remove from heat, stir in a a half or a full cup of heavy cream
- Serve

### Soup Addon

The soup above is not complete. One of two additional approach can be taken when making this soup.

#### Option 1

- Cube up 3-4 large potatoes
- Add to the soup recipe along with rabe

#### Option 2

- While the soup is simmering cook you pasta of choice
- Toss a handful or two of pasta in a bowl before serving soup
